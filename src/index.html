Direct Automation vs. Manual Tooling

Why Not Just Copy and Paste into ChatGPT?

ChatGPT can help write a reply. ReplyVera manages the ongoing review workflow.

Drafting text is only a fraction of review management. The real challenge is monitoring Google daily, copying reviews back and forth, enforcing safety rules, and never letting negative feedback slip through unchecked.

Auto-Publish Routine Positive Reviews No (must write each reply) No (must generate & paste each) Yes (eligible praise auto-published)
Sensitive-Review Detection Dependent on staff judgment Only if manually prompted per review Automatic risk & keyword flags
Human Approval Workflow Ad-hoc / unmanaged Manual review in chat interface 1-click approval for negative reviews
